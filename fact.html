5 FACTS ABOUT FORMULA 1

The fastest ever speed in a race is 231.5 miles per hour

In the world of Formula 1 speed is the name of the game. These powerful cars can certainly achieve that. The fastest speed ever recorded in a Formula 1 race is a mind-blowing 231.5 miles per hour by Montoya who was driving for McLaren-Mercedes. This incredible feat is all because of the advanced engineering, aerodynamics, and the power packed into these incredible racing cars.

The sound of a Formula 1 car is around 130 decibels

That is louder than a rock concert. These cars can produce a noise level of around 130 decibels, which is equivalent to standing next to a military jet during takeoff. Loud noise above 120 decibels can cause harm to your ears so it is important to protect them!

The tyres are specially designed to grip the track

Formula 1 tyres are perfectly made for the job. They are specially designed to grip the track so well that if you touch them, they feel super sticky, almost like a giant piece of chewing gum! The tyres of a Formula 1 car can also get extremely hot during a race. In fact, they can reach temperatures of up to 100 degrees celsius.

The sleek design of the car helps to cut through the air faster

Formula 1 cars are built with a sleek design for a specific reason, to cut through the air more efficiently. This streamlined shape helps the cars move through the air with less resistance. It is a bit like when you ride a bike and tuck in to go faster. The cars design is all about reducing the force of the air pushing against them. So, when Formula 1 cars race, their aerodynamic shape allows them to slice through the air smoothly, making them faster and more agile on the track. It is a smart engineering move that plays a big role in the speed and performance of these incredible racing machines.

Driver’s experience g-force!

When Formula 1 drivers zoom around the track, they go through a wild experience called g-force. The “g” in g-force stands for gravity. Imagine you’re on a super-fast rollercoaster… it’s that feeling of being pushed to the side really hard, but even more intense! In Formula 1, the cars go so fast and turn so sharply that the drivers feel like they’re getting squished! So, when you see those Formula 1 drivers taking tight turns on the track, they’re actually experiencing some super intense forces!
