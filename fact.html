5 FACTS ABOUT FORMULA 1

Record Race Speed: 231.5 mph

Driven by Juan Pablo Montoya for McLaren-Mercedes, this all-time F1 speed record was achieved through cutting-edge engine power and aerodynamics.

Ear-Splitting 130 dB Engine Noise

Louder than a rock concert and matching a military jet at takeoff, 130 dB exceeds the 120 dB threshold for hearing damage, making ear protection essential.

Ultra-Sticky Tyres Hotter Than Boiling Water

Engineered for extreme track grip, F1 tyres feel as sticky as chewing gum and reach blistering temperatures of up to 100°C during a race.

Aerodynamic Design Cuts Air Drag

Streamlined bodywork minimizes air resistance, allowing F1 cars to slice smoothly through the air for maximum speed and cornering agility.

Drivers Face Extreme G-Forces

High-speed cornering subjects drivers to intense g-forces, creating a crushing sideways push even stronger than an extreme rollercoaster.
